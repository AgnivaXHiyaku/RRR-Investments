Chapter II · Our research framework

श्रवण · मनन · निदिध्यासन

Three stages of knowing.

In the Upanishadic tradition, knowledge comes in three stages. First you hear it. Then you question it. Then you stay with it until it becomes certainty. Our research follows the same path. It’s how we turn information into conviction.

श्रवण

01 · Śravaṇa · Listen

Hear the market before it speaks.

Informed by market signals. We track and decode key regulatory and market triggers: NSE/BSE announcements, ESM/ASM alerts, government policies, and corporate acquisition news (including NARCL updates). These signals often provide the earliest hints of industry shifts.

  • NSE/BSE announcements
  • Regulatory frameworks like ASM/ESM
  • Government policy updates
  • Acquisition & restructuring news

The shankha is blown to announce what is coming. We listen for it first.

मनन

02 · Manana · Question

Test every belief.

Strengthened by ground work. Numbers tell one part of the story, people tell the rest. Our team leverages the scuttlebutt approach, speaking to suppliers, customers, and industry participants, to uncover truths that don’t appear in reports. We regularly meet company managements to test our hypotheses and align perspectives.

  • Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP)
  • Scuttlebutt checks across supply chains
  • Direct management meetings

Water in an urli only shows a true reflection once it is still. We let the noise settle before we look.

निदिध्यासन

03 · Nididhyāsana · Know

Stay with it until it becomes conviction.

Driven by agility. Markets reward adaptability. Using our C.O.R.E framework, we constantly test assumptions and reposition when required. This agility allows us to identify young, fast-growing businesses that are positioned for structural growth.

  • C.O.R.E framework application
  • Identifying early-stage growth
  • Validating long-term scalability

A diya’s flame stays steady in the wind. Conviction has to be earned. It can’t be borrowed.

Chapter III · The framework

C.O.R.E

Four lenses that every idea has to pass through before it earns a place in the portfolio.

C

Contrarian Lens

RRR Investments challenges consensus thinking to uncover opportunities others miss. We take positions only where facts outweigh sentiment and conviction is built on evidence, not market mood.

O

Original Insights through scuttlebutt

We generate differentiated insights by engaging with customers, suppliers, distributors and competitors. This ground-level intelligence helps us validate assumptions, understand real business momentum, and stay ahead of purely desk-based research.

R

Risk calibrated venture investing

We invest in early-stage growth stories in public and private markets. Our approach balances venture capital through disciplined position sizing and risk reward evaluation.

E

Edge through proxy and perception

We look beyond direct beneficiaries to identify proxy exposures, value-chain businesses poised to benefit indirectly. We also track sentiment and investor psychology to anticipate sentiment shifts ahead of price, creating a differentiated and durable edge.
